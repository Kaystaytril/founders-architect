The framework

Five pillars.
One business structure.

Founders Architect is organised around five connected pillars that bring the financial, statutory, people and founder layers of a business into one structure.

01 / FIN

Financial

Financial architecture and ledger services around the financial foundation of the business.

02 / TAX

Tax

Tax structure, preparation and compliance designed to reduce surprises and improve readiness.

03 / SEC

Secretarial

Company-secretarial and statutory compliance treated as part of the business architecture.

04 / PAY

Payroll & Labour

Payroll administration and labour compliance supporting the people side of the operating structure.

05 / FDR

Founder

Coaching and reset work aimed at clarity, confidence, direction and sustainable performance.

A company can only carry as much complexity as its underlying structure can support.

01 · Financial + tax

The commercial foundation.

Financial Architecture, the Ledger Collection and Tax Architecture form the central finance layer of the practice. Together they support visibility, recurring financial administration, tax compliance and the information a founder needs to make informed decisions.

“Tax should never be a surprise.” The approach is to prepare early, keep the structure current and avoid unnecessary last-minute reaction.

View ledger services ↗
02 · Secretarial + people

The operating structure.

Secretarial Architecture, Payroll Architecture and Labour Architecture cover the administrative and people-related systems that sit around a functioning company.

The Payroll Ledger includes payroll processing, payslips, employee onboarding and terminations, EMP201 and UI-19 submissions, bi-annual EMP501 submissions, IRP5 / IT3(b) certificates and annual COIDA ROE and LOGS.

View payroll scope ↗
03 · Founder

The leader behind the structure.

Founder Coaching sits under Founder Architecture, with once-off Strategy Sessions focused on a specific business challenge or decision and Monthly Coaching for ongoing coaching and accountability.

Founder Reset centres on sustainable high performance and the clarity required to lead over the long term.

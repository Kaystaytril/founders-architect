About the practice

Structure, clarity
and disciplined precision.

Founders Architect is a boutique financial architecture and advisory firm working with a select number of aligned founders in South Africa.

The premise

Business architecture, not disconnected admin.

Founders Architect brings tax strategy, financial structure and operational clarity into one connected advisory framework: Financial Architecture, Tax Architecture, Secretarial Architecture, Payroll Architecture, Labour Architecture and Founder Architecture.

The result is a practice designed to look at the systems around a founder as a connected whole — from recurring ledgers and compliance to payroll, business funding partners and founder coaching.

“Sustainable success is intentionally designed.”
